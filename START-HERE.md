# Start here

This folder is where your Launchhouse work lives from now on. Claude works in it with you, on your own computer, and it is saved to your own private copy on GitHub.

You might be wondering whether this is more setup than the app was. It is less. There is no key to paste, no passphrase and no database. You never open a terminal. Everything happens in apps you click through.

## 1. Install three things, once

1. **The Claude desktop app**, from claude.ai/download, signed in to your paid Claude account.
2. **GitHub Desktop**, from desktop.github.com, signed in to your GitHub account. No account yet? Make one free at github.com first. It is how this folder gets onto your computer, and how your work gets backed up.
3. **On a Windows PC only: Git for Windows**, from git-scm.com. Run the installer and press Next on every screen without changing anything. It is what lets Claude save your work and run the Launchhouse checks. A Mac does not need it, though the first time Claude saves your work a Mac may offer to install developer tools: press Install.

## 2. Take your own private copy

You did something like this for the app. This time it is an import rather than a fork.

1. Go to **github.com/new/import**, signed in to your GitHub account.
2. Where it asks for the address of the repository to import, paste `https://github.com/Philm-moxywolf/launchhouse-v3`. Leave the login boxes empty.
3. Give your copy a name, for example `launchhouse`.
4. Choose **Private**.
5. Press **Begin import**. It takes a minute or two, and GitHub shows you when it is done.

**Why private, and why not Fork?** Your Brain, your posts and your plans are saved in this copy, so only you should see it. A fork of our folder is always public. An import is the same folder, kept private.

**Updates still reach you.** The engine is a plugin, and you update it from the Plugins panel when we say. Nothing ever has to be pulled into your copy.

## 3. Get it onto your computer

1. On GitHub, on your new copy, press the green **Code** button, then **Open with GitHub Desktop**.
2. In GitHub Desktop, choose where to keep it, for example your Documents folder, and press **Clone**.

## 4. Open it in Claude

1. Open the Claude desktop app and choose **Code**.
2. Choose this folder. Always this same folder.
3. If Claude offers to install the Launchhouse plugin, say yes.

If it does not offer, press the **+** button next to the message box, choose **Plugins**, add the marketplace `Philm-moxywolf/launchhouse-v3`, and install **growth-engine**.

If you used the older Launchhouse toolkit in Claude, remove it now, before step 5: in **Plugins**, remove `growth-engine` from `launchhouse`, then quit and reopen the app. Your work in its folder is not touched. With both installed, the older one can answer "start launchhouse" instead.

## 5. Say "start launchhouse"

It checks your computer is ready, asks your name and where you are, and gets the folder ready. About two minutes.

## 6. Bring your work across

If you built anything in the Launchhouse app, bring it over once.

1. In the app, open **Files** and press the button that downloads everything.
2. Drag the downloaded file into this folder. Leave it zipped. If your Mac opened it into a folder called `growth-engine` instead, do not drag that folder in. Leave it where it is: Claude finds it at the next step.
3. Say **"bring my work across"**.

Your work arrives as it was. It gets tidied for the new setup, and nothing you wrote is rewritten without your yes. After this you do not need the app.

If you used the older Launchhouse toolkit in Claude instead, check you removed it at step 4. Then say **"bring my work across"** and tell Claude which folder that work is in. It is copied, never moved. If you are new and have nothing in the app or in Claude, say **"build my founder brain"** instead.

## 7. Connect your tools

In the Claude app, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**. Connect **HighLevel**, and **Apollo** if you sell to businesses. Then say **"connect my tools"** and Claude checks each one works.

## 8. Check

Say **"where am I up to"**. Claude reads your folder and tells you what is done and the one thing to do next.

## Backing up

Claude saves your work as you go. To send it up to GitHub, open GitHub Desktop and press **Push origin**.

Three things never go to GitHub, on purpose, because they hold real people's details: your `people` folder, `outreach-firstlines.csv` and `dm-openers.md`. They stay on your computer.

## The other folders in here

`plugins`, `scripts` and `tests` are the engine's own files. They came with the copy, and you can leave them alone. Your work lives in the `growth-engine` folder, which "start launchhouse" makes.

## Using Cowork as well

Cowork can work in this same folder. It is good for dropping in photos, documents and old posts, and for thinking things through. Anything saved into the `growth-engine` folder is seen by both.

## Stuck

Say what is happening in your own words, in Claude. If it is still not right after two tries, post in the Slack channel.
