# Pictures, clips and writing samples

What founders were asked to collect before the sessions, and where it goes.

## Where media goes

Media goes in the **GoHighLevel Media Library**. That is where posts are published from, so a picture anywhere else has to be moved before it can be used.

Before GoHighLevel was set up in Session 2, founders kept things somewhere they could find, to move across in one go.

## Consumers (B2C)

The visual track, so this is the bigger ask.

| What | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Short clips | 12 | 20 to 40 seconds each, filmed upright on a phone, good light, no editing |
| Photos | 20 | Upright and landscape. Their work, their product, their place, them |
| Logo | 1 | PNG with a transparent background if they have one |

## Businesses (B2B)

Lighter, because the posts are mostly words.

| What | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A photo of the founder | 1 | A decent one. It is what people check before replying |
| Logo | 1 | PNG with a transparent background if they have one |
| Screenshots or photos of the work | 5 to 10 | Optional. A dashboard, a result, a before and after |

## Writing samples, both tracks

**What.** Ten to twenty things the founder has already written: old posts from anywhere, a newsletter, a page of their website, an email they were pleased with, long WhatsApp or Slack messages. For consumers, old captions count and are often the best of it.

**When.** Before the content engine runs. Thirty pieces written before the voice is known read like a brochure.

**How many.** Five or six is enough to work from. Almost none is normal: the Founder Brain interviews them instead.

**Where.** Each piece goes in `growth-engine/voice-samples/`, one per file. It is the only folder read for voice.
